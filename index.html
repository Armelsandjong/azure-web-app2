Our approach

Technology in service of people

AEC approaches technology as a means, not an end. Systems should serve people, support sustainable growth and remain simple enough to be maintained over time.

Our advice always combines analytical method with human judgement — never one without the other.

About AEC
Founder

Armel Sandjong Nantchouang

Founder and Business Consultant, AEC.

Armel Sandjong Nantchouang founded AEC in 2017 as a grassroots initiative delivering technology solutions to businesses directly. He holds an MBA, is an alumnus of WSB University and works as an independent researcher; his master's thesis examined the impact of ERP systems on business performance. His published work is available on ResearchGate.

AEC now operates online with a network of trusted partners across Africa and Europe.
