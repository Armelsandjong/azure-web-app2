Our story

Started small. Never stopped.

I'm Armel Sandjong Nantchouang, founder of AEC. This is the honest version of how a grassroots idea in 2017 became a consultancy between Africa and Europe — and why I'm still going.

Book a free chat See my research
How it started

No spotlight. Just a start.

AEC didn't begin in a fancy office. In 2017 it was a grassroots initiative: delivering technology solutions hand to hand and door to door, helping one business at a time. I wrote project plans, listened to business owners, and learned by doing.

Today AEC works online with a network of trusted partners across Africa and Europe, with our base in Kraków and regional offices in Douala and Abuja. The way of working hasn't changed: listen first, explain clearly, and build something that genuinely helps.

Not everyone believed in it. Some people laughed. I kept learning, kept building, and kept showing up. — Armel

Learning never stopped

While AEC grew, I kept studying — in Cameroon and in Poland — because I wanted my advice to rest on real knowledge. Over the years that meant theses, research papers, certifications and an MBA.

It wasn't always easy, and not everyone understood why I kept going. But every step added something, and all of it counts.

Milestones

Seven years, one direction

You don't have to take my word for it. This is the road, step by step.

2017

The first project plans

I wrote my first project plans — a sheep, pig and fish farm, and a multi-sport complex — learning how to turn an idea into a clear plan. AEC began the same year.

2018 – 2019

People, money and ethics

I studied and wrote about human capital, financial markets and business ethics.

2020

Technology and organisations

A thesis on how technology and innovation affect organisational performance, and the annual report of the IT centre at the Université Internationale Jean Paul II de Bafang, Cameroon.

2022

Money at home

An analysis of household budgets — because good finance starts with ordinary families.

2023

A wider toolkit

Design thinking, IT systems for machine efficiency, stress management, and a study on the economic impact of Cameroonian immigration in Poland, published in the WSB Journal of Business and Finance. I also gathered my certifications in project management, Agile/Scrum, AI & ML, maths, statistics, banking and finance.

2024

From idea to defence

A market analysis for AEC (January), my master's thesis defence on how ERP affects business performance (March), two books of essays on life, happiness and growth (May and June), and my MBA defence (September).

Today

Still building

An independent researcher, MBA holder and WSB University alumnus, building AEC between Kraków, Douala and Abuja — and looking for a PhD programme.
