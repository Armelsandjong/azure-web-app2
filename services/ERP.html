The platform

About Dynamics 365 Finance

Dynamics 365 Finance is Microsoft's enterprise financial management application, part of the Finance and Operations apps. It brings an organisation's financial data and processes into a single system, supporting control, visibility and reporting across entities.

Our advisory work focuses on the functional areas listed here, where the quality of the initial design largely determines how well the system serves the organisation afterwards.

Functional areas of focus

  • General ledger, chart of accounts and financial dimensions
  • Accounts payable
  • Accounts receivable, credit and collections
  • Cash and bank management
  • Fixed assets
  • Budgeting
  • Consolidation and financial reporting

Other ERP platforms

Vendor-neutral evaluation

Our delivery focus is Dynamics 365 Finance. At the evaluation stage, we can also help you compare it with other platforms — for example Odoo, SAP S/4HANA or Oracle NetSuite — against your own requirements, so that the decision rests on your needs rather than on a vendor's preference.

Implementation of other platforms would be coordinated with specialist partners.
