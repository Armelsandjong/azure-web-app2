Build a Repeatable Month-End Close for Better IFRS Reporting

Dependable financial reporting starts with a close process people can repeat. A clear schedule and traceable review steps help finance teams prepare consistent information for decision-makers.

Set a close calendar with clear owners

List recurring close activities, dependencies, expected evidence, responsible owners, and reviewers. Work backward from the reporting deadline so teams know when upstream information is needed. Keep the calendar in a shared place and make changes visible.

Reconcile key balances consistently

Define which accounts require reconciliation, what supporting documentation is expected, and who reviews each item. Track unresolved differences to resolution rather than carrying unexplained balances forward. Consistent account ownership makes follow-up easier from period to period.

Connect source data to reported figures

Document how operational transactions flow into the general ledger and how ledger balances map into financial statements and management reports. Use stable account mappings and reporting dimensions. When data moves between systems, assign owners for validating completeness and investigating rejected or late records.

Make review controls part of the workflow

Separate preparation from review where practical. Record who completed each step, when it was reviewed, and what changed after review. A short, consistent evidence trail helps explain significant movements and supports more efficient internal and external review.

Use ERP reporting to reduce manual rework

Start with standard reports and well-governed data before adding custom dashboards. Agree on the definitions behind measures such as revenue, operating costs, and working capital. Automate repeatable extracts only after validating the source, timing, and reconciliation to the ledger.
